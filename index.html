Three levels

One lion. Three ways to colour it.

Each of the 300 subjects is drawn three times, from big friendly shapes to fine detail. Siblings can colour the same picture, each at their own level.

  • 3–5

    Little artists

    Big shapes and thick lines for small hands and chunky crayons.

  • 6–8

    Young explorers

    Picture-book drawings with more to discover and colour in.

  • 9+

    Creative masters

    Realistic, detailed pages for older kids, and for grown-ups who want a calm half hour.

Dot-to-dot

Join the dots. Then colour it in.

Every simple drawing also comes as a dot-to-dot. Counting from one dot to the next turns into a stegosaurus, a cat or a rocket, and then into a colouring page.

  • A dot-to-dot for each of the 300 subjects
  • Counting practice that ends in a picture
  • Prints just like a colouring page

Printing

Ready to colour in a minute.

  1. Pick a subject

    Browse by category or search: unicorn, car, dinosaur…

  2. Choose the level

    3–5, 6–8 or 9+. Or switch to the dot-to-dot.

  3. Print

    Send it to the printer at home. Any AirPrint printer works.

  • Works with any AirPrint printer
  • A4 or US Letter, picked for your country
  • Print a whole set as a book
