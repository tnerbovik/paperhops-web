Free colouring pages for a screen-free moment

Swap 20 minutes of screen for a page to colour. These 36 free printable colouring pages and 12 dot-to-dots are for parents and grandparents who want a calm, screen-free activity with the children: print a stack before the grandkids visit, and there is always something ready on the table. They come at three levels, from three-year-olds to grown-ups, and the 9+ pages double as relaxing adult colouring to do side by side.

Less screen time, more colouring

  • Hands-on

    Holding a crayon, staying inside the lines and choosing colours trains the small hand movements children later use to write.

  • Calm, without notifications

    A sheet of paper doesn’t ping, autoplay or ask for one more minute. Children settle into it at their own pace.

  • Side by side

    The detailed 9+ pages are relaxing adult colouring too. Sit down together, each with your own page.

  • Ready in a minute

    Print a stack while the kettle boils, before the grandkids arrive. No account, no sign-up, no ads.
