Who owns the drawings

Every colouring page, dot-to-dot and illustration in Paperhops, and the app itself, belongs to Paperhops. The app gives you a licence to use them as described here; it does not hand over ownership.

What you may do

Print as many copies as you like for your own family and for children you look after, and colour them. Teachers and kindergarten staff may print copies for their own group of children. Coloured-in pages are yours to keep, hang up and give away.

What you may not do

Sell the pages, printed or as files. Share or upload them as files, for example on websites, social media, in shared folders or in other apps. Use them in products for sale, in other books or collections, or to train AI models. Remove the Paperhops name from them.

Paperhops Pro

Paperhops Pro is a licence for the person who buys it and their family (through Family Sharing). It unlocks printing of the whole library under the same terms as above. Purchases are also covered by Apple’s standard licence agreement for apps from the App Store.

How the drawings are made

The drawings are created with the help of AI image tools from our own descriptions, then picked, checked and cleaned up by hand before they are added to the library.
